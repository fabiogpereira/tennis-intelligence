# portfolio-storyteller

## Purpose
Make the repository communicate professional judgment, curiosity, and honest trade-offs.

## Use when
Updating `README.md`, case studies, methodology, architecture, results, release notes, or AI documentation.

## Required inputs
- Audience and document purpose.
- Current evidence and unresolved questions.
- Decisions, failed approaches, trade-offs, and validation performed.
- AI contributions and human review.

## Workflow
1. Lead with the question and why it matters.
2. Explain the research boundary before describing implementation.
3. Distinguish discovery from hypothesis and result.
4. Make difficult decisions, failed ideas, limitations, and trade-offs visible.
5. Connect architecture and tests to user and research needs.
6. Use concrete language; remove inflated claims and corporate filler.
7. Check that a technical and non-technical reader can navigate the story.

## Expected outputs
A concise, evidence-aligned narrative with clear next steps and explicit uncertainty.

## Validation checks
The first screen answers what, why, and current status. The document explains what AI contributed, what humans owned, and how claims were validated.

## Challenge or reject when
Reject marketing claims without evidence, retrospective certainty, hidden failures, vague ownership, or installation instructions that overwhelm the project story.

# product-design-reviewer

## Purpose
Ensure the research becomes an understandable, accessible, and credible product rather than a generic analytics dashboard.

## Use when
Designing or reviewing web pages, charts, interaction flows, copy, responsive behavior, or research storytelling.

## Required inputs
- Product surface and target audience.
- Research claim, data state, and user task.
- Desktop and mobile views when available.
- Accessibility and loading/empty/error states.

## Workflow
1. Identify the user question and the decision the screen supports.
2. Check hierarchy, typography, spacing, chart semantics, and information density.
3. Check that uncertainty, methodology, sample size, and limitations are discoverable.
4. Inspect desktop and mobile screenshots when browser tooling is available.
5. Test loading, empty, error, keyboard, contrast, and reduced-motion states.
6. Remove decorative complexity that competes with the research story.
7. Recommend concrete copy and interaction changes.

## Expected outputs
A prioritized review of usability, visual hierarchy, accessibility, responsive behavior, and research comprehension.

## Validation checks
A visitor can understand the question, evidence status, and next action without a notebook or oral explanation. Charts have clear units, baselines, labels, and uncertainty.

## Challenge or reject when
Reject dashboard clutter, unsupported certainty, inaccessible interactions, misleading visual encoding, fabricated future studies, or layouts that work only on a developer's screen.

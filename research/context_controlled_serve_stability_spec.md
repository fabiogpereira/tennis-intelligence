# Context-controlled serve stability specification

**Experiment ID:** `research-context-serve-stability-v0.1`

**Status:** pre-specified internal falsification experiment; no player output

## Question and estimand

**PROJECT HYPOTHESIS:** candidate serve rates may capture repeatable player behavior within the
charted MCP sample after basic observed match context is held approximately constant.

The estimand is the ratio between median same-player split-sample distance and median different-
player distance within the same tour and context stratum. It is descriptive of the pinned charted
sample. It is not a causal player effect, a talent estimate, or a universal playing-style trait.

## Candidate families

The experiment repeats the already versioned definitions and distances without changing their
denominators:

1. `serve_outcomes`: mean absolute distance across first-serve-in, ace, and double-fault rates;
2. `first_serve_direction`: mean deuce/ad total-variation distance across wide/body/T shares; and
3. `second_serve_direction`: the same conditional direction distance for second serves.

Passing one family does not approve another and does not approve a composite Tennis DNA vector.

## Pre-specified context dimensions

| Dimension | Stratum definition | Primary threat |
|---|---|---|
| Surface | Exact normalized MCP surface | Surface-driven serve mix |
| Era | Calendar decade | Equipment, court, and charting drift |
| Player ranking | `1-10`, `11-25`, `26-50`, `51-100`, `101-200`, `201+`, `missing` | Changing player level |
| Opponent ranking | Same fixed bands | Opponent-quality confounding |
| Tournament | Exact normalized MCP tournament | Event-specific conditions and selection |
| Chart author | Exact case-preserving MCP author, blank explicit | Contributor conventions |
| Joint basic context | Surface + era + opponent-ranking band | Several simple explanations jointly |

Ranking bands are fixed before results and are sensitivity strata, not publication tiers.

## Split, eligibility, baseline, and uncertainty

- Sort each player-stratum group by match date and match ID, then split chronologically in half.
- Evaluate 2 and 5 matches per half. This grid is sensitivity analysis, not an eligibility rule.
- Require valid feature denominators in both halves.
- Compare each same-player distance with all different-player comparisons available in the same
  tour and stratum.
- Report eligible player-strata and distinct players separately so repeated surface/tournament
  strata do not masquerade as additional players.
- Resample matches independently within each half for a deterministic 50-replicate bootstrap range
  of the median same-player distance. Treat it as a diagnostic range, not a confidence interval.

## Falsification and interpretation

For a family and context dimension, a within/between ratio at or above one fails the repeatability
check. Ratios below one are necessary but not sufficient evidence. A family is weakened when the
ratio materially approaches or crosses one relative to the aggregate pilot, eligible coverage
collapses, chronological evidence is inconsistent across tours, or results are concentrated in one
author, tournament, surface, or era.

No single cutoff automatically passes a family. Null, negative, sparse, and contradictory results
remain in the report. No rankings, player vectors, clusters, similarity map, or public profile may
be generated by this experiment.
